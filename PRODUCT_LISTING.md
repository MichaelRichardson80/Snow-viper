### What Is the Snow Viper?

The **Snow Viper** is a patented, built-and-tested, next-generation cooling system based on a **water-to-air inner cooling architecture combined with CO₂ (carbon dioxide) refrigerant technology**. It has been physically constructed and proven in testing, and is now ready for mass manufacturing.

The invention addresses key limitations of conventional cooling systems by integrating two proven heat-exchange principles into a compact, efficient design — making it ideal for racing, high-performance automotive, HVAC, and industrial applications.

---

### Key Features & Benefits

- 🌡️ **Water-to-Air Heat Exchange** — Efficient heat transfer from liquid to air medium
- ❄️ **CO₂ Inner Cooling** — Uses CO₂ (R-744) as a natural, low-GWP refrigerant for environmentally friendly operation
- ⚙️ **Built & Tested** — A working prototype has been physically built and validated
- 🔒 **Patented** — Full patent protection; the IP is ready for outright sale or licensing
- 🏁 **Motorsports & Automotive Ready** — High-performance design suited for race cars, performance street builds, and OEM vehicles
- 🌍 **Wide Application Range** — Suitable for HVAC, automotive/EV thermal management, data centers, industrial equipment, and refrigeration
