### What Is the Snow Viper?

The **Snow Viper** is a patent-pending, next-generation cooling system based on a **water-to-air inner cooling architecture combined with CO₂ (carbon dioxide) refrigerant technology**. It is engineered to deliver superior thermal management efficiency across a wide range of industrial, commercial, and automotive applications.

The invention addresses key limitations of conventional cooling systems by integrating two proven heat-exchange principles into a compact, efficient design — making it suitable for mass manufacturing.

---

### Key Features & Benefits

- 🌡️ **Water-to-Air Heat Exchange** — Efficient heat transfer from liquid to air medium
- ❄️ **CO₂ Inner Cooling** — Uses CO₂ (R-744) as a natural, low-GWP refrigerant for environmentally friendly operation
- ⚙️ **Ready for Manufacturing** — The design is production-ready and scalable for OEM integration
- 🔒 **Patent Pending** — Protected intellectual property with licensing and sale opportunities available
- 🌍 **Wide Application Range** — Suitable for HVAC, automotive/EV thermal management, data centers, industrial equipment, and refrigeration
