# Snow Viper
**Patent Pending | Water-to-Air Inner Cooling CO₂ Cooling System**

The Snow Viper is an innovative cooling system that leverages water-to-air heat exchange combined with CO₂ inner cooling technology, delivering highly efficient temperature management for a wide range of applications.

### 6. Approaching a Company: Recommended Steps

1. **Prepare a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)** before sharing detailed technical information.
2. **Create a one-page executive summary** that covers: the problem solved, how Snow Viper solves it, market size, and patent status.
3. **Request a meeting with the R&D or licensing department** — avoid general sales inboxes.
4. **Demonstrate a prototype or technical validation data** if available.
5. **Work with a patent attorney** to understand your licensing options (exclusive vs. non-exclusive) before negotiating.
